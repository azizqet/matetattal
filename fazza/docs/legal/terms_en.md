Terms & Conditions — FAZZA (فزّاع)
(Draft — requires legal review before publication)

1. Definitions
"FAZZA" or "the Platform": the app, dashboard and related services. "Customer": a person requesting a delivery. "Driver": a person providing delivery services after FAZZA approval. "Order": a delivery request created by a customer. "Offer": the price and time proposed by a driver.

2. Nature of the service
FAZZA is an online intermediary that connects customers and drivers within the State of Kuwait. FAZZA does not perform deliveries itself and does not set delivery prices; the driver proposes the price and the customer freely accepts it. The delivery agreement is made directly between the customer and the driver.

3. Eligibility and accounts
- Users must be 18 or older and use a valid Kuwaiti phone number that belongs to them.
- One account per phone number. A phone number is shown as "not verified" until FAZZA staff actually verify it.
- You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for all activity on your account. We do not send SMS codes; password recovery is handled by support after identity verification.

4. Creating orders
- Customers must describe orders accurately, set correct locations and attach genuine photos.
- Prohibited: illegal items, alcohol and drugs, weapons and ammunition, hazardous or flammable materials, live animals, large amounts of cash or valuable jewellery, and anything requiring a special licence.
- Drivers may refuse suspicious orders; FAZZA may cancel violating orders and suspend accounts.

5. Offers, acceptance and payment
- Customers receive and compare driver offers; accepting one automatically declines the others.
- The delivery fee is paid by the customer directly to the driver in cash or any method they agree on. FAZZA does not collect delivery fees.
- The value of goods bought by the driver on the customer's behalf (if any) is paid by the customer to the driver and is never commissioned.

6. Cancellation
- Customers may cancel free of charge before accepting an offer.
- After acceptance and before pickup, customers may cancel with a reason; the cancellation is recorded.
- Drivers may withdraw from an accepted order before pickup only, with a reason; the order returns to receive new offers.
- After pickup, cancellation is only possible through support.
- After delivery the customer confirms receipt; if not confirmed within the period shown in the app, the order is completed automatically.

7. Driver terms
- Drivers must provide accurate, valid documents (Civil ID, driving licence, vehicle registration and any permits required to operate) and be approved before receiving orders.
- Drivers must comply with Kuwaiti traffic, labour and residency laws and insure their vehicles.
- Activation fee: a one-time amount set by FAZZA (currently KWD 5). There is no monthly subscription.
- Commission: a percentage of the delivery fee on completed orders only, set by FAZZA (currently 15%); never charged on purchases.
- When unpaid commissions reach the limit (currently KWD 5), new orders pause automatically; accepted orders can still be completed. Receiving resumes after payment and admin confirmation.
- Payment receipts are uploaded in the app and are approved only after manual admin review.
- Fees may change with prior notice in the app and never retroactively for accepted orders.

8. Conduct, ratings and reports
Everyone must act respectfully: no abuse, harassment or fraud. Each party may rate the other and report users or conversations. FAZZA reviews reports and may hide abusive ratings and suspend accounts.

9. Liability
FAZZA is a technology intermediary and is not responsible for order contents, damage, loss or delays caused by the parties, while it uses reasonable care to vet drivers and handle complaints. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be excluded by law.

10. Suspension and account deletion
FAZZA may suspend accounts that breach these terms. Users can delete their account in the app or via the deletion request link; outstanding dues remain payable.

11. Governing law
These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Kuwait, and the courts of Kuwait have jurisdiction.

12. Changes and contact
We may update these terms and publish the new version in the app. Contact us from the Support page in the app.

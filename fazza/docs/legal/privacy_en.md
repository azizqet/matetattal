Privacy Policy — FAZZA (فزّاع)
(Draft — requires legal review before publication)

1. Data we collect
- Account data: name, phone number, password (stored hashed by the authentication provider; we never see it), optional profile photo, language.
- Order data: pickup and drop-off locations, details and photos, recipient details if you enter them, status history, ratings.
- Location: your location when choosing addresses or viewing nearby requests (a driver's location is not stored for browsing requests). A driver's location is shared and stored only during an active order and deleted afterwards. We do not collect location in the background.
- Driver data: Civil ID, driving licence, vehicle registration, permits and vehicle details, stored in private storage accessible only to the driver and authorised review staff.
- Driver financial data: commission and payment history and payment receipt images.
- Chats, reports and support tickets.
- Technical data: device push token and security logs (e.g. failed sign-in attempts).

2. Purposes
Operating the service and matching customers with drivers, live tracking during orders, notifications, support and report handling, driver vetting, commission accounting, fraud prevention and account protection, and legal/accounting obligations. We do not use your data for targeted advertising, do not track you across other apps, and do not sell your data.

3. Sharing
- Between customer and driver: only after an offer is accepted does each party see the other's name and phone number; the driver sees the full address and recipient details; the customer sees the driver's location during the order. Before acceptance, drivers see only the pickup point, order details and an approximate drop-off area.
- Service providers: hosting and database (Supabase), notifications (Expo / Apple / Google), maps (Apple Maps / Google Maps), bound to protect the data.
- Authorities, when legally required.

4. Security
Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), row-level security on every table, private document storage with short-lived links, sign-in attempt limiting, and an audit log of all admin actions.

5. Retention
We keep account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, personal data, photos and documents are deleted; financial and order records are kept anonymised for the period required by accounting and legal rules.

6. Your rights
You can view and edit your data in the app, delete your account from Account → Delete account, via the external deletion request link, or by contacting support.

7. Children
The service is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes and contact
We may update this policy and publish the new version in the app. Contact us from the Support page in the app.

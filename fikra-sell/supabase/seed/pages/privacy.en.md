# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

FIKRA SELL respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, how we protect it and the rights you have.

## What we collect

- **Account data:** name, e-mail address and password (stored hashed; nobody can read it).
- **Optional data:** profile photo, bio and the phone number you choose to show on your ads.
- **Content you create:** ads, their text and photos, in-app messages, reports and support requests.
- **Approximate location:** only if you allow it, to show nearby ads or locate your ad. It is rounded (about 100 m) before it is stored, and we never track you in the background.
- **Device data:** a push token to deliver the notifications you enabled, and your app language.
- **Usage statistics:** aggregated counts (such as ad views) that do not identify you.

We do not collect payment data at this time, we never sell your data, and we do not use it for third-party targeted advertising.

## How we use it

- To run your account, publish your ads and enable buyer–seller chat.
- To send the notifications you choose (messages, ad status, saved-search matches, offers).
- To keep users safe, fight fraud and spam, and review reported content.
- To improve the app using aggregated statistics.

## What other people see

- Visible to everyone: your ads and photos, your name and profile photo, and your phone number **only** if you enable calls or WhatsApp on an ad.
- Your e-mail address is **never** shown to other users.
- Private messages are visible only to the two participants. Moderators can open a conversation only after it is reported, and only to verify the report.

## Service providers

Data is hosted by Supabase (database, storage and authentication). Notifications are delivered through Expo and Apple/Google push services. These providers process data only to deliver the service.

## Security

Encrypted connections, strict database-level permissions that stop any user from reading another user's data, limits on sign-in and posting attempts, and sessions stored in your device's secure storage.

## Retention and deletion

- You can edit your data or delete any ad at any time.
- You can **permanently delete your account inside the app** (Account → Manage account → Delete account). This immediately removes your account, ads, photos, messages and favourites and cannot be undone.
- Old notifications are deleted automatically after 120 days.
- We may keep a minimal record (such as a deleted account ID and date) where required by law or to prevent abuse.

## Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your rights

You can access, correct and delete your data, and withdraw location or notification permission from your device settings at any time. For privacy questions use "Contact support" inside the app.

## Changes

We may update this policy and will let you know inside the app about any material change.

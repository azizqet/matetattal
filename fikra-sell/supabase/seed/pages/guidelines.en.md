# Community Guidelines

We want a safe, trusted marketplace for everyone. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.

## Not allowed

- Weapons, ammunition, explosives and their parts.
- Drugs, controlled substances and prescription medicines.
- Alcoholic beverages.
- Counterfeit, stolen or untraceable goods.
- Endangered or protected animals, or any animal whose trade is prohibited by law.
- Pornographic or indecent content, or anything that exploits children.
- Official documents, ID cards, bank accounts and personal data.
- Counterfeit money, fraud tools, gambling and betting.
- Misleading or fake ads, or ads asking for advance payment without a real item.
- Hate speech, discrimination, threats or incitement to violence.
- Anything prohibited by the laws of the State of Kuwait.

## Ad quality

- A clear title and real photos of the actual item.
- The right category and a real price.
- No duplicate posting (the system rejects duplicates automatically).
- Mark your ad as "Sold" once it is sold.

## Respect

- No abuse, harassment or repeated unwanted messages.
- Never ask for passwords, verification codes or card details.

## Reporting and blocking

- Report any ad, user or conversation with the "Report" button. Block any user to hide their content and stop them contacting you immediately.
- Our moderators review reports within 24 hours, remove violating content and ban offenders.
- Ads that receive several reports are hidden automatically until reviewed.

Breaking these guidelines can lead to content removal and a permanent ban.
